P-001 / Flagship / In development

FOCUS

A compact carry system built as hardware: a 3D-printed monocoque shell, deliberate tolerances and a recessed flush-fit inlay. The design explores how additive manufacturing can create a small object with fewer visual seams and a strong material identity.

Development is ongoing. Public details remain intentionally limited while geometry, materials and production methods are validated.
