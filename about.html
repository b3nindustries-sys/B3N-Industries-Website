About B3N / Bennett's Brilliant Notions

Engineering ideas
into useful reality.

B3N Industries is a Caribbean-rooted engineering, invention and product development studio. We help inventors, creators and small businesses move from an uncertain idea to a clear design, working prototype or digital experience.

Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
Trinidad & Tobago roots
Built for practical progress

What B3N Industries does

We combine product design, CAD, rapid prototyping, mechatronics and manufacturing-minded development with websites and interactive 360° tours. That mix lets B3N work across physical and digital systems without losing sight of the person who has to use them.

Our work includes engineering consulting, prototype development, original products such as the FOCUS Wallet, practical tools such as BENCH and immersive property experiences through SPACES.

Our mission

Turn the imaginary into real things—and make serious engineering support more understandable and accessible, especially for independent inventors and Caribbean innovators.
